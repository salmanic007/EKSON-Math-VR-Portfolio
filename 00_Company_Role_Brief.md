
## 1. Role Understanding

The role combines mathematics expertise, educational content development, curriculum understanding, and collaboration with technology and instructional design teams.

## 2. What I Do in the Role

1. Analyze the mathematics curriculum and identify the required learning outcomes.

2. Define clear and measurable learning objectives for each lesson.

3. Design explanations, examples, activities, questions, and assessments that match the learner's level.

4. Review mathematical content to ensure accuracy, clarity, and alignment with the curriculum.

5. Create scripts and storyboards that explain what the learner sees, hears, and does.

6. Define interactive, visual, 3D, or VR elements that can make the mathematical concept easier to understand.

7. Work with instructional designers, developers, and 3D/XR teams to turn the educational design into an interactive experience.

8. Test the learning experience and identify content, mathematical, usability, and instructional issues.

9. Provide feedback and quality assurance before the lesson is released.

## 3. Learning Design Framework

Every lesson should answer six questions:

1. OBJECTIVE — What should the learner learn?

2. EXPERIENCE — What should the learner see and experience?

3. INTERACTION — What should the learner do?

4. FEEDBACK — What happens when the learner is correct or incorrect?

5. ASSESSMENT — How do we know the learner achieved the objective?

6. QA — How do we verify that the mathematics, content, instructions, and interaction are correct?

## 4. Portfolio Projects

The portfolio will contain three mathematics learning experiences:

1. Grade 5 — Fractions
   Focus: visual learning, comparing and adding fractions, and interactive representations.

2. Grade 8 — Volume of 3D Shapes
   Focus: 3D visualization, spatial understanding, measurement, and interactive problem solving.

3. Grade 12 — Functions and Transformations
   Focus: graphs, transformations, mathematical reasoning, and problem solving.
