
# Curriculum Analysis: Grade 5 - Fractions

## 1. Topic Overview
- **Subject:** Mathematics
- **Grade:** 5
- **Topic:** Adding and Comparing Fractions (Like Denominators)

## 2. The Hook (Introduction)
Instead of starting with numbers on a board, we start with a real-life scenario:
"A chocolate bar divided into 8 equal squares. You ate 2 squares (2/8), and your friend ate 3 squares (3/8). How much did we eat together?"

## 3. Learning Objective
By the end of this lesson, the student will be able to:
- Visually represent fractions using a 3D model
- Add fractions with like denominators by combining visual parts
- Compare two fractions with the same denominator

## 4. VR/3D Opportunity
- **Interaction:** The student can "hold" the chocolate bar, break off pieces, and see them combine in real-time
- **Visualization:** Seeing that 2/8 + 3/8 = 5/8 physically, not just numerically
