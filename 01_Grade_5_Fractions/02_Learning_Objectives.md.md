
# Learning Objectives: Grade 5 - Fractions

## By the end of this VR lesson, the student will be able to:

### Knowledge Level (Remember & Understand)
1. **Identify** the numerator and denominator in a fraction
2. **Explain** what the denominator represents (total parts)
3. **Explain** what the numerator represents (selected parts)

### Application Level (Apply & Analyze)
4. **Represent** a fraction visually using a 3D model (chocolate bar)
5. **Add** two fractions with like denominators by combining physical pieces
   - Example: 2/8 + 3/8 = 5/8
6. **Compare** two fractions with the same denominator
   - Example: Which is greater: 3/8 or 5/8?

### VR-Specific Objectives
7. **Interact** with 3D objects to manipulate fractions
8. **Visualize** the concept of "whole" vs "parts"
9. **Receive** immediate feedback on their answers

## Success Criteria
The student demonstrates mastery when they can:
- Correctly add 3 out of 3 fraction problems
- Explain why 5/8 is greater than 3/8 using visual evidence
- Complete the lesson in under 15 minutes
