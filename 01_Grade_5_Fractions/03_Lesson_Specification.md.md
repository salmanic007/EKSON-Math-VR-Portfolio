
# Lesson Specification: Grade 5 - Fractions

## 1. General Information
- **Lesson Title:** The Chocolate Bar Challenge: Adding & Comparing Fractions
- **Target Audience:** Grade 5 Students (Ages 10-11)
- **Estimated Duration:** 15-20 Minutes
- **Difficulty Level:** Beginner / Introductory

## 2. Prerequisites
- Basic understanding of what a fraction is (numerator/denominator).
- Ability to use basic VR controllers (pointing, grabbing).

## 3. VR Environment & Setting
- **Scene:** A cozy virtual kitchen table.
- **Lighting:** Bright, warm, and inviting (daylight).
- **Main Object:** A large, realistic 3D chocolate bar divided into 8 equal squares.
- **UI Elements:** Minimalist floating text for instructions and feedback.

## 4. Tools & Interactions
- **VR Headset:** Standard 6DoF headset (e.g., Meta Quest).
- **Controllers:** Used to grab, move, and snap chocolate pieces together.
- **Gaze Interaction:** Looking at an object for 2 seconds to highlight it.

## 5. Assessment Strategy
- **Formative:** Immediate feedback after each interaction (visual + audio cues).
- **Summative:** A final "Challenge Mode" where the student must solve 3 problems independently to "unlock" the chocolate bar and eat it.
