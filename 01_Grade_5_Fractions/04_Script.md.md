
# Script: Grade 5 - The Chocolate Bar Challenge

## Scene 1: Introduction (The Hook)
**Visual:** 
The student appears in a cozy virtual kitchen. A large, delicious 3D chocolate bar is on the table, divided into 8 equal squares.

**Narrator (Friendly Voice):** 
"Welcome! Look at this chocolate bar. It's divided into 8 equal pieces. Today, we're going to solve a delicious math problem."

**Student Action:** 
The student looks around the kitchen.

---

## Scene 2: First Interaction
**Visual:** 
A glowing outline appears around the first 2 squares of the chocolate bar.

**Narrator:** 
"Imagine you are very hungry and you eat 2 pieces. Can you grab those 2 pieces and put them on your plate?"

**Student Action:** 
The student uses the VR controller to grab the 2 highlighted squares and moves them to a virtual plate.

**Feedback (Success):** 
The squares snap to the plate. A small text appears: "You ate 2/8".

---

## Scene 3: Second Interaction
**Visual:** 
Now, 3 different squares glow with a different color.

**Narrator:** 
"Your friend joins you and eats 3 pieces. Please grab those 3 pieces and add them to your plate."

**Student Action:** 
The student grabs the 3 new squares and places them next to the first ones on the plate.

**Feedback (Success):** 
The squares snap to the plate. Text updates: "Friend ate 3/8".

---

## Scene 4: The Core Question
**Visual:** 
The camera zooms in on the plate, which now holds 5 squares total. The original chocolate bar is still visible in the background with 3 squares missing.

**Narrator:** 
"Look at your plate. You ate 2 pieces, and your friend ate 3 pieces. How many pieces did you eat together? And what fraction of the whole bar is that?"

**Student Action:** 
A floating UI panel appears with options:
- A) 5/16
- B) 5/8
- C) 6/8

**Interaction:** 
The student selects option B (5/8).

---

## Scene 5: Conclusion & Feedback
**Visual:** 
If Correct: The chocolate bar glows green, and confetti appears.
If Incorrect: The narrator gently guides the student to count the pieces again.

**Narrator (Correct Path):** 
"Excellent! 2/8 plus 3/8 equals 5/8. You can see it right here on your plate. You've mastered adding fractions!"
