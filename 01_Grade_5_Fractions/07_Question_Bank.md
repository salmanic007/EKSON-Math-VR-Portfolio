        <div class="question-card">
            <span class="question-type">Question 1: Conceptual Understanding (VR Interaction)</span>
            <h3>The Chocolate Bar</h3>
            <p><strong>Question:</strong> Look at the chocolate bar on the table. If the whole bar is divided into 8 equal pieces, and you grab 3 pieces, what fraction do you hold?</p>
            
            <div class="answer-box">
                <span class="label">✅ Correct Answer:</span> 3/8
            </div>
            
            <div class="distractor-box">
                <span class="label">❌ Distractors:</span>
                <ul>
                    <li>3/5 (Confusing parts with the remaining pieces)</li>
                    <li>8/3 (Inverting numerator and denominator)</li>
                </ul>
            </div>
            
            <div class="feedback-box">
                <span class="label">💬 Feedback (Correct):</span> "Exactly! 3 parts out of 8 total parts."
                <br><br>
                <span class="label">💬 Feedback (Incorrect):</span> "Remember, the bottom number (denominator) is the total pieces in the whole bar, and the top number (numerator) is the pieces you are holding."
            </div>
        </div>

        <div class="question-card">
            <span class="question-type">Question 2: Adding Fractions (Visual)</span>
            <h3>Adding Pieces Together</h3>
            <p><strong>Question:</strong> You have 2/8 of a chocolate bar on your plate. Your friend adds 4/8 more. How much chocolate is on the plate now?</p>
            
            <div class="answer-box">
                <span class="label">✅ Correct Answer:</span> 6/8
            </div>
            
            <div class="distractor-box">
                <span class="label"> Distractors:</span>
                <ul>
                    <li>6/16 (Adding denominators together - common misconception)</li>
                    <li>8/8 (Thinking it makes a whole automatically)</li>
                </ul>
            </div>
            
            <div class="feedback-box">
                <span class="label">💬 Feedback (Correct):</span> "Great job! 2 pieces plus 4 pieces makes 6 pieces out of 8."
                <br><br>
                <span class="label">💬 Feedback (Incorrect):</span> "When adding fractions with the same denominator, the denominator stays the same. We only add the top numbers (numerators)."
            </div>
        </div>

        <div class="question-card">
            <span class="question-type">Question 3: Comparing Fractions (VR Pointer)</span>
            <h3>Which is More?</h3>
            <p><strong>Question:</strong> Which fraction represents MORE chocolate: 5/8 or 2/8?</p>
            
            <div class="answer-box">
                <span class="label">✅ Correct Answer:</span> 5/8
            </div>
            
            <div class="feedback-box">
                <span class="label">💬 Feedback (Correct):</span> "Correct! Since the pieces are the same size, having 5 pieces is more than having 2."
                <br><br>
                <span class="label">💬 Feedback (Incorrect):</span> "Look at the virtual models. Which pile of chocolate is bigger? 5 pieces or 2 pieces?"
            </div>
        </div>

        <div class="question-card">
            <span class="question-type">Question 4: Challenge / Application</span>
            <h3>What's Left?</h3>
            <p><strong>Question:</strong> The whole chocolate bar is 8/8. If you eat 5/8, how much is left?</p>
            
            <div class="answer-box">
                <span class="label">✅ Correct Answer:</span> 3/8
            </div>
            
            <div class="distractor-box">
                <span class="label">❌ Distractors:</span>
                <ul>
                    <li>3/0</li>
                    <li>13/8</li>
                </ul>
            </div>
            
            <div class="feedback-box">
                <span class="label">💬 Feedback (Correct):</span> "Brilliant! 8 pieces minus 5 pieces leaves 3 pieces."
                <br><br>
                <span class="label">💬 Feedback (Incorrect):</span> "Start with the whole bar (8 pieces) and take away the 5 pieces you ate. Count what is left."
            </div>
