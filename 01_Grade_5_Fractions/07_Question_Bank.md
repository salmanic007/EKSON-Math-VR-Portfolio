
# Question Bank: Grade 5 - Fractions

## Question 1: Conceptual Understanding (VR Interaction)
**Question:** Look at the chocolate bar on the table. If the whole bar is divided into 8 equal pieces, and you grab 3 pieces, what fraction do you hold?
**Correct Answer:** 3/8
**Distractors:** 
- 3/5 (Confusing parts with the remaining pieces)
- 8/3 (Inverting numerator and denominator)
**Feedback (Correct):** "Exactly! 3 parts out of 8 total parts."
**Feedback (Incorrect):** "Remember, the bottom number (denominator) is the total pieces in the whole bar, and the top number (numerator) is the pieces you are holding."

## Question 2: Adding Fractions (Visual)
**Question:** You have 2/8 of a chocolate bar on your plate. Your friend adds 4/8 more. How much chocolate is on the plate now?
**Correct Answer:** 6/8
**Distractors:** 
- 6/16 (Adding denominators together - common misconception)
- 8/8 (Thinking it makes a whole automatically)
**Feedback (Correct):** "Great job! 2 pieces plus 4 pieces makes 6 pieces out of 8."
**Feedback (Incorrect):** "When adding fractions with the same denominator, the denominator stays the same. We only add the top numbers (numerators)."

## Question 3: Comparing Fractions (VR Pointer)
**Question:** Which fraction represents MORE chocolate: 5/8 or 2/8?
**Correct Answer:** 5/8
**Distractors:** 
- 2/8
**Feedback (Correct):** "Correct! Since the pieces are the same size, having 5 pieces is more than having 2."
**Feedback (Incorrect):** "Look at the virtual models. Which pile of chocolate is bigger? 5 pieces or 2 pieces?"

## Question 4: Challenge / Application
**Question:** The whole chocolate bar is 8/8. If you eat 5/8, how much is left?
**Correct Answer:** 3/8
**Distractors:** 
- 3/0
- 13/8
**Feedback (Correct):** "Brilliant! 8 pieces minus 5 pieces leaves 3 pieces."
**Feedback (Incorrect):** "Start with the whole bar (8 pieces) and take away the 5 pieces you ate. Count what is left."
