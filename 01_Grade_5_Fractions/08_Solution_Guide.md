
# Solution Guide & Misconceptions: Grade 5 - Fractions

## 1. Step-by-Step Solution for Core Problem
**Problem:** Add 2/8 + 3/8
- **Step 1:** Identify the denominators. Both are 8. (They are "like" denominators).
- **Step 2:** Keep the denominator the same (8).
- **Step 3:** Add the numerators: 2 + 3 = 5.
- **Step 4:** Write the final fraction: 5/8.
- **Step 5:** Verify visually: Count the total pieces on the virtual plate (5 pieces) out of the original whole (8 pieces).

## 2. Common Misconceptions & How to Address Them in VR
| Misconception | Why students make it | VR Feedback / Correction Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Adding denominators:** (2/8 + 3/8 = 5/16) | Student applies a rote rule of "adding top and bottom" without understanding the concept of the "whole". | **Visual Proof:** Show the student that the chocolate bar still only has 8 pieces total, not 16. The denominator represents the whole, which didn't change. |
| **Comparing denominators:** (Thinking 2/8 is bigger than 5/8 because 8 is big) | Confusing the size of the "whole" with the size of the "parts". | **Visual Proof:** Place the 2-piece stack and the 5-piece stack side-by-side in VR. Ask: "Which pile is physically taller?" |
| **Ignoring the "Whole":** | Student forgets what the denominator refers to. | **Narrator Prompt:** "Remember, the bottom number is the total pieces the *entire* bar was cut into." |
