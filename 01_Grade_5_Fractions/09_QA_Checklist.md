
# QA Checklist: Grade 5 - Fractions (The Chocolate Bar Challenge)

## 1. Mathematical Accuracy (✅ Must be 100% Correct)
- [ ] The chocolate bar is visually divided into exactly 8 equal parts.
- [ ] The fractions displayed (2/8, 3/8, 5/8) correctly match the visual representation.
- [ ] The addition logic (2/8 + 3/8 = 5/8) is mathematically sound and clearly explained.
- [ ] No typos in numbers, fractions, or mathematical terms (Numerator/Denominator).

## 2. VR Interaction & Usability
- [ ] The "Grab and Drop" mechanic works smoothly without lag.
- [ ] The "Snap-to-grid" feature activates reliably when pieces are near the plate.
- [ ] The VR pointer accurately selects the multiple-choice options.
- [ ] The experience does not cause motion sickness (camera movements are smooth and controlled).

## 3. Instructional Design & Feedback
- [ ] The "Hook" (introduction) is engaging and clearly stated within the first 30 seconds.
- [ ] Immediate feedback is provided for both correct and incorrect answers.
- [ ] The feedback for incorrect answers is constructive and guides the student to the right answer (no harsh "Wrong!" messages).
- [ ] The lesson can be completed within the estimated 15-20 minutes.

## 4. UI/UX & Accessibility
- [ ] Text is large, high-contrast, and easy to read in the VR headset.
- [ ] Highlighting colors (e.g., for selected pieces) are distinguishable for colorblind users.
- [ ] Audio narration is clear, well-paced, and synchronized with the visual events.
