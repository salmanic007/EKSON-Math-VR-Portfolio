Company Role Brief

1. Role Understanding

The role combines mathematics expertise, educational content development, and collaboration with technology and instructional design teams.

2. What I Do in the Role

  1. Analyze the mathematics curriculum and identify the required learning outcomes.
  2. Define clear and measurable learning objectives for each lesson.
  3. Design explanations, examples, activities, questions, and assessments that match the learner's level.
  4. Review mathematical content to ensure accuracy, clarity, and alignment with the curriculum.
  5. Create scripts and storyboards that explain what the learner sees, hears, and does.
  6. Define interactive, visual, 3D, or VR elements that can make the mathematical concept easier to understand.

3. Collaboration Points

  • Work with 3D Artists to create accurate mathematical models
  • Collaborate with Unity Developers to implement interactions
  • Coordinate with Instructional Designers to ensure pedagogical effectiveness
  • Partner with QA Testers to verify mathematical accuracy
